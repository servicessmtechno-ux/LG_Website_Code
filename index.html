Washing Machine Service

LG Washing Machine Service Center in Hyderabad

Our experienced technicians repair every type of LG washing machine — top load, front load and fully automatic. Whether your machine isn't spinning, not draining, showing error codes, making strange noises or leaking water, we diagnose and fix all types of problems at your doorstep.

🔍 Common Issues We Repair

  • Washing machine not starting
  • Not spinning or agitating — motor, drive belt or lid switch
  • Water drainage problems or stuck cycle
  • Error codes like UE, OE, LE and display malfunctions
  • Excessive noise or vibration during wash cycles
  • Water leakage from door, pump or hoses
  • Water inlet problems — valve & filters
  • Door lock issues and overheating

🔧 Services We Provide

  • Complete diagnosis and troubleshooting
  • Motor and drum repairs or replacement
  • Water drainage and pump repairs
  • Control board and electrical repairs
  • Water seal and leakage repair
  • Error code resolution (UE, OE, LE)
  • Suspension, inlet valve and hose repairs
  • Genuine LG part replacement
